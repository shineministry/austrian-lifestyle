Austrian Etiquette

🤝
Greetings: Use "Gruss Gott" as a universal greeting. Shake hands when meeting someone. Use "Sie" (formal you) unless invited to use "Du".
punctuality
Punctuality is sacred. Arriving late is considered very rude. Being 5 minutes early is the norm. Germans call it "Punktlichkeit" — Austrians take it even more seriously.
🚪
Doors & Elevators: Always hold the door for the person behind you. In elevators, greet everyone with "Gruss Gott" when entering.
🍷
Coffee house culture: Sit as long as you like in a Cafe. But never rush a waiter — order at the table. Tipping: round up 5-10%.
🗣️
Language: German is official. Many Austrians speak dialect (Wienerisch). Basic German phrases earn huge respect. "Danke" and "Bitte" go a long way.
🏠
Visiting homes: Bring a small gift (flowers, chocolate, wine). Remove shoes at the door — look for the Schuhschrank (shoe cabinet).

Transport Tips

🎫

Annual Ticket (Klimaticket)

E995/year for ALL public transport nationwide — trains, buses, trams, U-Bahn. The best deal in Europe. Under 26 pays ~E525.

🚇

Vienna U-Bahn

Runs 5AM-12:30AM. Night buses (N lines) run all night on weekends. Single ticket: E2.40. 24h pass: E8. Always validate your ticket!

🚌

Regional Buses (Postbus)

Connect rural areas. Buy tickets online at oebb.at or from the driver (surcharge). Check the Postbus app for schedules.

🚄

Trains (OBB)

Westbahn competes with OBB on Vienna-Salzburg route — often cheaper! Book Sparschiene tickets early for huge discounts.

🚗

Driving Rules

Drive on the right. Headlights mandatory 24/7. Winter tires required Nov-Apr. Vignette (highway sticker) mandatory — buy at gas stations or online.

🚲

Cycling

WienRadl in Vienna, many cities have bike-sharing. Helmets required for under-12. E-bikes are hugely popular. Use marked bike lanes.

Recycling Guide

📦
Gelbe Tonne (Yellow)
Plastic bottles, cans, tetrapacks, plastic packaging, metal lids, aluminum foil
📄
Blaue Tonne (Blue)
Paper, cardboard, newspapers, magazines, paper bags, egg cartons
🍶
Grune Tonne (Green)
Glass bottles (green & brown), jars — NO white glass, NO ceramics
🍂
Biomull (Brown bin)
Food scraps, fruit/veg peels, coffee grounds, garden waste, eggshells
🔋
Sondermüll (Special)
Batteries, electronics, paint, chemicals — bring to designated drop-off points
🗑️
Restmüll (Gray bin)
Non-recyclable waste: dirty packaging, hygiene products, broken ceramics
